# ProCare Weekly Staff Schedule — Operation Plan

## Data-Driven Optimization

Based on analysis of **3,370 invoices over 5 years** of sales data.

### Peak Hours Findings

| Time Window | % of Daily Sales | Staffing Required |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| 8 AM - 12 PM | 16.5% | 1 staff (quiet) |
| **12 PM - 8 PM** | **71.1%** | **2 staff minimum** |
| 8 PM - 1 AM | 12.4% | 1 staff (quiet) |

**Peak hour**: 6:00 PM (10.5% of all invoices)
**Secondary peaks**: 1:00 PM (10.1%), 7:00 PM (9.9%), 5:00 PM (9.5%)
**Slowest day**: Friday (10.9% of weekly sales)

## Staff Profiles & Task Assignment

### Dr. Nour — صيدلانية امتياز (Pharmacy Intern)
- **Shift**: Morning 8 AM - 4 PM
- **Solo window**: 8-11 AM only (3 hours max, quiet period)
- **Tasks**: Receiving orders, shelf organization, customer service, daily count
- **Restrictions**: Cannot dispense controlled substances alone

### Mrs. Nada — صيدلانية مرخصة (Licensed Pharmacist)
- **Shift**: Midday 11 AM - 7 PM
- **Overlap with Nour**: 11 AM - 4 PM (supervision)
- **Tasks**: Prescription dispensing, controlled substances, supervising Nour, clinical consultations
- **Key role**: Bridge between morning quiet and afternoon peak

### Mr. Abdalla — صيدلي مرخص (Licensed Pharmacist)
- **Shift**: Afternoon 3 PM - 11 PM
- **Overlap with Nada**: 3-7 PM / **Overlap with Youssef**: 5-11 PM
- **Tasks**: Shift handover, shortage orders, branch transfers, evening sales
- **Key role**: Links day team to night team

### Dr. Youssef — صيدلي مرخص (Licensed Pharmacist)
- **Shift**: Night 5 PM - 1 AM
- **Overlap with Abdalla**: 5-11 PM, then solo 11 PM - 1 AM
- **Tasks**: Narcotics/controlled dispensing, pharmacy closing, night sales, end-of-day reports
- **Key role**: Responsible for closing and cash reconciliation

---

## Coverage Guarantees

1. Pharmacy open 8 AM - 1 AM every day including Friday
2. Minimum 2 staff during peak hours (12 PM - 8 PM) every day
3. Nour never alone more than 3 hours
4. Licensed pharmacist always on duty (Nada/Abdalla/Youssef)
5. Abdalla-Youssef overlap covers the busiest evening window
6. Friday (slowest day) has 3 staff but adjusted schedules
7. Days off staggered — never two from same team off together
