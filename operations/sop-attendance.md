# SOP-001: Attendance & Departure — الحضور والانصراف

**Effective Date:** September 2026
**Scope:** All pharmacy staff — Branch 1 & Branch 2
**Owner:** Pharmacy Manager

---

## 1. Purpose

Ensure accurate, tamper-proof attendance tracking for all pharmacy staff using dual-verification: manual eStock registration + automatic CCTV timestamps.

## 2. Attendance Registration (eStock)

Every employee MUST:

1. **Clock in** on the eStock system at the start of their shift
2. **Clock out** on the eStock system at the end of their shift
3. Register using their **own account** — never use another employee's login
4. Register **before** beginning any pharmacy duties

### Late Registration

- If an employee forgets to clock in/out, they must notify the manager within the same shift
- The manager will add a manual entry with a note explaining the reason
- Repeated failures to register (3+ per month) result in a written warning

## 3. CCTV Verification — كاميرات المراقبة

- Security cameras automatically record all entries and exits
- Camera timestamps serve as the **authoritative record** in case of disputes
- Camera footage is reviewed weekly by management
- If eStock registration time and camera timestamp differ by more than **15 minutes**, the camera time is used

## 5. Grace Period

- **5 minutes** grace period for clock-in (e.g., morning shift: 8:00-8:05 AM)
- After grace period, the employee is marked **late**
- 3 late arrivals in one month = 1 absence deduction

## 6. Absence Policy

- Planned leave: request **48 hours** in advance via manager
- Sick leave: notify manager **before** shift start + provide medical certificate within 24 hours
- Unexcused absence: salary deduction + written warning

## 7. Violations & Consequences

| Violation | First | Second | Third |
|-----------|-------|--------|-------|
| Forgot to register on eStock | Verbal reminder | Written warning | Salary deduction |
| Late arrival (after grace) | Noted | Written warning | 1-day deduction |
| Buddy punching (using another's account) | Written warning | 3-day suspension | Termination |
| Unexcused absence | 1-day deduction | 3-day deduction | Termination review |
